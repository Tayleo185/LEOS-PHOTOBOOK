# Leo 7.4 — Business Insights

Owners, administrators and bookkeepers can open Business Insights in each company. Reviews run on demand using accessible company records, with a warning when pagination leaves records unloaded. No external AI provider or new database permissions are used.

Includes month-to-date profit comparisons with prior-month calendar cutoffs, overdue invoice/bill alerts, spending growth, expense budgets, missing attachments, possible duplicates, trucking loss/cost-per-mile review and up to three prioritized actions. Recommendations explain their evidence and link to existing pages. No automatic changes, messaging, tax determinations or forecasts. Weekly delivery is not scheduled. Future and archived records are excluded; overdue alerts span all dated history.

37 automated tests pass. Original database and receipts are unchanged.
