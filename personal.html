About this version

Version 6.4 adds settlement audits, digital inspections, truck maintenance, document expiry reminders, and private document attachments. It preserves private route-and-fuel planning, protects all accounts with private records and receipt storage, and stops inserting any other business’s sample loads into new accounts. Your existing records remain in cloud storage. Photo recognition runs on your device using an OCR library downloaded when scanning; reviewed scans can match the same load by its pickup, load or delivery reference number; cloud-connected records, extracted text and compressed receipt images sync to your private Supabase account.

Important: review every scanned rate and delivery record before it changes your books. A signed POD suggests a completed delivery, while a settlement statement alone does not verify that a bank deposit arrived. When the cloud is configured, signed-in verified accounts synchronize through Supabase. Offline or unverified accounts remain local-only; no cloud backup occurs until synchronization is active.
